Mean |SHAP| is the average distance a feature moves a score from the baseline, ignoring direction (so pushes up and pushes down do not cancel out). The signed average lets pushes up and down cancel, so it is usually small; it says nothing about whether more of a feature means more risk (for that, look at individual explanations). These are patterns in a model trained on simulated labels: associations, not causes, and not validated on real households.
