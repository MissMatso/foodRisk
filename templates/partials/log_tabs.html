Audit logs

Why these logs are kept and when they are needed
  • Accountability: every change to a household record, and every account change, shows who did it and when.
  • Explaining a changed score: if a household's score moved, the log shows which details were edited, by whom, and when.
  • Investigating mistakes or misuse: unexpected deletions, edits by an account that should not make them, or repeated failed sign-ins.
  • Data protection (POPIA): the NGO can show that personal information is only handled by authorised people. The log records which fields changed, never the old or new values, so it is not a second copy of personal data.

The logs are read-only: no page in the system edits or deletes a log entry. They are kept for as long as the records they describe.
