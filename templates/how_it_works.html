THE WORKFLOW

From a household record to a clearer next step.

FoodRisk brings household information and assessment history into one place so NGO teams can review needs with context.

Sign in to the platform ↗

THREE CONNECTED STEPS

A practical workflow for a small team.

01 / REGISTER

Capture the household

Authorised staff record contact details, composition, income, and access to essentials. The form checks required values and household counts before saving.

02 / ASSESS

Generate an indicative score

A staff member requests an assessment. FoodRisk stores the score, category, model version, and feature contributions alongside the household record.

03 / REVIEW

Plan follow-up

Staff inspect household history and the risk dashboard. Analysts can view reports. A person reviews the result before any support decision.

BUILT AROUND RESPONSIBILITIES

The right view for each role.

Accounts are created by the system administrator; there is no public household registration.
