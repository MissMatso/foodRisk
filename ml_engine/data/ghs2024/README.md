# GHS 2024 source files

Statistics South Africa. General Household Survey 2024 [dataset]. Version 1. Statistics SA [producer], 2025; DataFirst [distributor], 2025. DOI: https://doi.org/10.25828/rx6h-3r34.

Public-use, edited/anonymised data under CC-BY (attribution). Source: https://www.datafirst.uct.ac.za/dataportal/index.php/catalog/1027. Household and person DTA files were extracted unchanged from the user-supplied GHS archive. metadata.pdf and questionnaire.pdf document official variables/codes. Model manifests record exact source hashes. Derived mappings are our modelling choices, not official Statistics SA risk scores. See docs/DATA_DICTIONARY.md.
